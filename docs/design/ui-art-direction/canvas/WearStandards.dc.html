Budget and build
One hero damage mark on screen at a time. At most two W2 surfaces in view; everything else is W1 or clean.
Text holds 4.5:1 measured on the worn texture, not the flat colour. If wear breaks it, the wear moves.
Elements under 120 px on their short side get no texture, only edge tone.
Wear is seeded per object and never animates or reshuffles between frames.
Ship it as nine-slice paper: wear lives in the corners and edges, the centre slice is flat paper the shader tints.
Wear can carry state over time: the atlas yellows a step every few days, and a driver's tag picks up a tear per injury.
