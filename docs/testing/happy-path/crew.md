# Crew

1. On the compound, press the Crew tab: the atlas sheet opens on "Crew and decks", the first driver's card marked in highlighter in the driver pool.
2. Look at the middle box: the driver's name as the compound says it ("Road Warrior 1"), their vehicle, HP, hand limit, deck and Ready chips, their deck as minis with Remove under each, and the cost curve.
3. Press another driver's card: the highlighter moves to it and the middle box shows their deck.
4. After a run has brought cards home, look at the locker: its cards as minis with Add and Scrap; a card for another archetype is faded.
5. Press Add under a locker card: one copy moves into the deck, and the counts on both sides change.
6. Press Remove under the same card: the copy goes back to the locker.
7. Press Scrap: it reads Confirm, with "Press again to scrap" in red; press it again a moment later: one copy goes, and the scrap in the note goes up by 5.
8. Press Scrap, then Escape: it lets go and reads Scrap again.
9. Press the Attack tab in the locker's filter: it's marked in highlighter, and only attack cards show.
10. Remove cards until the deck holds 8: every Remove turns off with "Deck at minimum" under its card.
11. Press Escape: the compound opens.
