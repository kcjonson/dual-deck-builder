# Battle: a fight on the road

1. Found a campaign, plan a run, load out two drivers, and drive to the first fight: the road fills the middle in the dark theme, raiders on the right half, your two vehicles in your inside lane, the dock with two hands below and End Turn at its right end.
2. Read the top bar: "Wave 1 of N", the turn, the last log line, and a LOG button; each raider's token has intent pills along its top.
3. Point at a playable card: it straightens and comes to the front, and arrows run over the road from your vehicle to what it would hit.
4. Drag an attack card onto a raider: legal raiders show dashed outlines and range chips (R1, R2, OUT), the one under the card goes solid with a ghost on its bars; release, and the hit pops a number off its plate and lands in the ticker.
5. Drag a card and release it on the top bar: nothing plays and the card goes back.
6. Tab to a card and press Enter: it lifts onto the nearest target; arrow keys move it between targets; Enter plays it there, Escape puts it back.
7. Drag Juke (or any card that moves you) onto an empty slot: your vehicle swerves there.
8. Drag Strafe onto a slot: the card waits with the raiders it can then reach outlined; press one and it fires from the new slot; Escape at the second step puts it back unplayed.
9. Point at End Turn: each intent draws a line to the vehicle it will hit.
10. Press End Turn: the dock drops and greys, End Turn reads WAIT, RAIDERS' TURN crosses the road, and about a second later the raiders act one at a time, the acting one glowing, its hits popping and the bars dropping as each lands.
11. Wait: YOUR TURN shows, the bars match the log, a new hand is dealt, and the dock comes back up.
12. Press L, then LOG: the log drawer opens and closes over the right of the road, its lines tagged by turn.
13. Win the fight (or `/api/dev/battle-play-out`): FIGHT WON stays over the road and the End Turn column shows Leave the fight.
14. Press Leave the fight: the battle result opens, then Back on the road returns to the run.
