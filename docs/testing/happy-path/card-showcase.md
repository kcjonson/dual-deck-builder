# Card showcase

1. On the main menu, press Card Showcase: the atlas sheet opens with Menu and Card showcase tabs, the title, and "47 cards".
2. Look at The decks: deck A's back is orange, deck B's teal, each with the W in its shield at the centre.
3. Rest the pointer on the first face under Sizes and states: after half a second its detail view opens beside it, with keyword boxes on its right.
4. Move off it: the view closes.
5. Right-click the same card: the view stays open and its foot says PINNED; right-click again: it closes.
6. Look at the minis: the stack shows two edges and "x5", Borrowed has a dashed frame and "+1", Left at home is faded with HOME, Locked has LOCKED.
7. Scroll the sheet with the wheel, then Page Down and Page Up: every card's face shows, then each rarity's minis; no summary runs past three lines.
8. Press the Menu tab: the main menu opens.
