# Load out and Customize

1. On the run route, pick a route and press Load out the crew: the atlas sheet opens on "Load out" with the destination, road, stops, fuel and home-by under the title.
2. Look at the seats: two drivers sit in them, each with their card, Clear seat, Customize, and their deck as minis; Start run is live, with "Both drivers take their default decks." beside it.
3. Press Clear seat on Driver 2: the seat says to choose a driver, Start run turns off with "Seat two drivers to start the run."
4. Press Seat under a free driver in the pool: they take Driver 2; an injured driver's Seat is off, with "Injured, fit in N days" under the card.
5. With escorts in the convoy, press one: it fades with STAYING and At home; press it again: it's marked Coming in highlighter.
6. Press Customize on Driver 1: Customize opens on "Driver 1 of 2", the escorts' cards LOCKED in the left column.
7. Press - under a card: a HOME stack appears beside it, faded, and LEFT HOME counts 1.
8. Press Borrow under a locker card: a dashed +1 stack joins the run deck, and BORROWED counts 1.
9. Press Give to driver 2 under an escort card: it leaves the column.
10. Press Reset to default: the HOME and +1 stacks go back, and the line says it's the default deck again.
11. Press - again, then Done: load out shows Driver 1's card with CUSTOM, and "One run deck is customized for this run."
12. Press Back to the route: the run route opens; press Load out the crew again: the decks are the defaults again.
13. Press Start run: the run opens on its first stop.
