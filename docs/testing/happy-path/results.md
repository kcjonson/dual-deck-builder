# Results: a fight's result, the debrief, and the campaign's end

1. Found a campaign, plan a run to a POI whose route has an ambush, load out two drivers, and drive to the ambush.
2. Win the fight (or `/api/dev/battle-play-out`) and press Leave the fight: the atlas sheet says "Fight won", "The road is clear", and After the fight lists who went down or the haulers' pay, or "Everyone drove on as they were."
3. Press Back on the road: the run is back, at the stop after the fight.
4. Drive the run home: the debrief says "Home from" and the POI's name, with LOOTED stamped beside it and the cargo in one line under it ("It brought home 6 food, 4 water, 5 fuel, and Headshot.").
5. Look at The crew: a driver who came home hurt says "injured, fit in N days".
6. Press Back to the compound: Needs names the same driver "fit in" the same N days, and the log's newest line is "A run came home with" the same cargo.
7. Plan another run and lose its fight (drop both drivers): the debrief says "Lost on the road to" the POI in red, stamped LOST, with the dead and missing and the cargo under Lost with the run.
8. With nobody left at the compound, press The end: the sheet's top-right corner is burnt, FALLEN is struck beside "The compound" and how it fell, and The last day ends on the fall's line with a red diamond.
9. Press Main menu: the menu opens with nothing to continue.
