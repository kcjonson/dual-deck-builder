# List of Similar Deckbuilder Roguelike Games

This list is compiled from research to identify games similar to Slay the Spire, Monster Train, and Balatro, for the purpose of designing a new game in the genre.

1. **Slay the Spire**: A foundational game in the genre, known for its tight mechanics, distinct characters, and high replayability.
2. **Monster Train**: Features a unique multi-story battlefield and a focus on unit placement and lane defense, in addition to deckbuilding.
3. **Balatro**: A poker-inspired roguelike deckbuilder that has gained massive popularity for its addictive gameplay loop and creative synergies with Joker cards.
4. **Wildfrost**: A high-quality deckbuilder with a unique companion and positioning system, and a focus on managing cooldowns.
5. **Vault of the Void**: Aims to reduce RNG by allowing players to see upcoming challenges and adjust their decks accordingly before encounters.
6. **Deliverance & Reign**: A dark fantasy themed deckbuilder with no energy restrictions for playing cards, and a unique scoring system based on treasure cards. It also has a mode similar to Monster Train.
7. **Zoeti**: A visually appealing, story-driven deckbuilder that uses standard playing cards to form poker hands, which then activate skills.
8. **Pirates Outlaws**: A Slay the Spire-like game with a pirate theme, featuring island-to-island navigation and unique pirate-themed mechanics.
9. **Night of the Full Moon**: Inspired by Dream Quest, this game features 1v1 card battles against enemies who also draw and play cards. It has a dark fairytale theme.
10. **Meteorfall: Journeys**: A mobile-friendly deckbuilder with a swipe-based interface for playing or skipping cards, focusing on stamina management.
11. **Dawncaster: Deckbuilding RPG**: A story-driven deckbuilder with multiple classes, synergies, and DLC content.
12. **Cobalt Core**: A sci-fi themed deckbuilder with a time-loop narrative, turn-based space battles, and ship customization.
13. **Pyrene**: Features a unique Basque setting and gameplay where players move from card to card on a board, resolving encounters. Strong narrative focus.
14. **Griftlands**: A deckbuilder where negotiation and combat are handled through separate decks, with a focus on emergent sci-fi storytelling.
15. **Luck Be a Landlord**: Described as deckbuilder-adjacent, this game involves building a collection of symbols for a slot machine to create synergies and earn rent.
16. **Inscryption**: A spooky, meta-narrative-driven deckbuilder with an escape-room feel and unique card mechanics like sacrifices.

This list provides a good starting point for analyzing common mechanics, successful innovations, and potential areas for improvement in the deckbuilder roguelike genre.
